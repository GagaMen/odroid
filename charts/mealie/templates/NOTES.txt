================================================================================
IMPORTANT: Initial Setup
================================================================================

On its first start Mealie creates an admin with a well-known login:

  changeme@example.com / MyPassword

Log in and change email and password before anything else -- until then,
anyone who can reach the ingress can log in as admin.
